Pearl Diver

Base humanoid rig, skinned mesh and authored animations:
Quaternius, Universal Animation Library, Standard edition.
License: CC0 1.0. See LICENSE.txt in this directory.
Official source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
Download: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
Retrieved September 29, 2026.

Bubble City modifications:
Pearl ceramic and teal materials, helmet/visor, illuminated collar,
breathing equipment and chest crest; arm geometry derived from the same
skinned body for first person. Body corkscrew combines authored pose
animation with a custom continuous axial turn, water sheath and bubbles.

The animation is inspired by the user's Warframe movement reference.
It is not Warframe's original animation asset or an exact reproduction.

The runtime file pearl-diver.avatar is a glTF binary container under a
non-model extension so source assets are not indexed as extra worlds.
The public edition includes the runtime assets and their CC0 license.

Three.js GLTFLoader and BufferGeometryUtils: Three.js r160, MIT license.
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160/examples/jsm

September 30, 2026 city traversal expansion:
- Added Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2 Standard (CC0), downloaded from the author's official itch.io page.
- Source: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2
- Runtime extended-motions.avatar is its GLB animation container, renamed to avoid duplicate model-library indexing.
- 40 curated authored emotes across both libraries; custom wall-contact inverse kinematics for continuous climbing; original ClimbUp_1m used for mantling.
- Hovering uses the authored FoldArms pose plus restrained body motion. No Warframe, Fortnite or licensed superhero animation was extracted or represented as an original game asset.
